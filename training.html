Core Requirement

Summer Training

The institute provides excellent training opportunities and encourages students to undertake summer training in reputed industrial organisations with stipends wherever applicable.

01

Secure Internship Placement

AIM's dedicated Internship Cell collaborates with industries, consultancy firms, and research organisations to identify suitable internship opportunities for every student.

02

Work on a Defined Project

Each student is assigned a specific project under the guidance of a senior manager at the host organisation. Projects cover live business challenges relevant to the student's specialisation.

03

Submit Internship Report

Upon completing the 10-week training, students submit a detailed internship report to their assigned faculty mentor. The report is evaluated for quality, analysis, and professional presentation.

04

Presentation & Evaluation

Students present their internship learnings and project findings before faculty panels. This develops communication skills and reinforces practical understanding of business operations.

Field Exposure

Industrial Tours

Both short visits and extended industrial tours are organised to provide practical exposure while offering opportunities for experiential learning beyond the classroom.

🏭 Manufacturing Sector

Visits to leading manufacturing plants and industrial hubs, allowing students to witness operational excellence, production management, and supply chain practices in action.

💻 IT & Technology Sector

Tours to IT companies and technology parks exposing students to software development lifecycles, project management methodologies, and digital business strategies.

🏭 Finance & Banking

Visits to banks, NBFCs, and financial institutions providing hands-on understanding of financial operations, credit management, and regulatory frameworks.

👥 NGO & Social Enterprises

Exposure to non-profit organisations and social enterprises developing students' understanding of CSR, impact measurement, and socially responsible management.

🛍 Retail & Marketing

Visits to retail chains, marketing agencies, and consumer goods companies providing practical insights into brand management, merchandising, and consumer behaviour.

🏠 Government & Public Sector

Exposure to government organisations and public sector undertakings developing students' understanding of public administration, policy implementation, and governance.

Skill Development

Case Writing

AIM has developed a strong case research culture. During the last year, faculty and students published case studies and technical notes used across various MBA courses.

The Research and Publications Committee formulates policy for case research and encourages faculty to develop rich, real-world case studies that bring business dilemmas into the classroom.

Case writing workshops are conducted regularly to help students and faculty master the art of identifying, researching, and writing business cases for academic and practitioner use.

Problem identification and framing
Field research and data collection
Case structuring and narrative writing
Teaching notes and discussion guides
Publication in academic journals and repositories
