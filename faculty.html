Dr. Sanjay Payasi

Director, Anand Institute of Management, Bhopal

Dr. Sanjay Payasi is a distinguished academician and professor at Anand Institute of Management in Bhopal. His career, spanning over two decades since its inception in 2004, is marked by significant contributions to both academia and administration.

Dr. Payasi's academic journey is comprehensive, holding multiple master's degrees in Mathematics, Personnel Management, and Marketing Management, and a Doctor of Philosophy in Management. He became a founder faculty member, significantly shaping various educational institutions and departments.

He has authored 24 books in Mathematics, Computer Applications, and Business Management, and holds a patent to his credit. Dr. Payasi is an approved research supervisor in management at Barkatullah University, Bhopal. He has served as an expert on the Research Degree Committee and a member of the Board of Studies in 23 different Central Universities and State Public Universities across 12 states and 2 Union Territories of India.

As an educator, Dr. Payasi is known for his expertise in advanced analytical tools and software, such as MS Excel, SPSS, Python, Tableau, Power BI, and Qlik. He is also the Chairman of Gulab Shiksha Samiti Bhopal and serves as the Secretary of the Indian Society for Training & Development (ISTD) Bhopal Chapter.
