Final Placements

Recruitment Process

A structured, transparent, and cohort-based placement system ensuring a smooth experience for both students and recruiting organisations.

01

Pre-Placement Talk

Companies conduct a pre-placement talk (PPT) to introduce themselves, share job roles, and answer student queries. PPTs may be held between September and October.

02

Student Shortlisting

AIM provides a filtered candidate database matching the recruiter's criteria. The institute can shortlist candidates on behalf of the company prior to interviews.

03

Aptitude Tests & GDs

Written aptitude tests and group discussions conducted on campus as part of the selection process, depending on recruiter requirements.

04

Personal Interviews

Interviews conducted on campus or at a mutually agreed location. AIM facilitates all logistics including interview rooms, student coordination, and scheduling.

05

Offer & Joining

Selected students receive offer letters. Final placements conclude by March. AIM coordinates joining formalities and follows up to ensure smooth onboarding.
