Campus Overview

A Campus That Inspires

Strategically located in Neelbad, Bhopal — a vibrant ecosystem designed to inspire creativity, foster leadership, and accelerate personal growth.

Campus at a Glance

Student life at AIM is designed to be dynamic, creative, and enriching. The Students' Activity Centre (SAC) serves as the hub of extracurricular engagement, offering facilities for clubs, gymnasium, music rooms, exhibition hall, and indoor games.

Beyond academics lies a vibrant campus experience filled with laughter, animated discussions, late-night brainstorming sessions, music performances, dance practices, cultural festivals, and friendly sports matches.

"The dorm is like the Gaulish village in the Asterix books — where every day may begin with lively debates but always ends in celebration."

— An AIM Alumnus

Student Governance

Student Committees

Student-led committees function as vital links between students, faculty, corporates, alumni, and media — providing real-world management experience.

Placement Committee

💼

Acts as a bridge between students and recruiters. Coordinates summer internships, final placements, and organises guest lectures and industry interaction sessions.

✉ placement@aim-bhopal.org

Web Committee

💻

The digital face of AIM — manages the official website, intranet (AIMBuzz), email systems, Wi-Fi infrastructure, and overall IT support for the campus.

✉ web@aim-bhopal.org

Strategym Committee

🏆

Organises Strategym — AIM's Annual Business Convention, providing students a platform to apply classroom knowledge to real-world business challenges.

✉ strategym@aim-bhopal.org

Alumni Relationship Committee (ARC)

🎓

Strengthens ties between AIM and its alumni through mentorship programmes, short-term projects, guest lectures, and campus engagements.

✉ arc@aim-bhopal.org

Corporate Relations Committee (CRC)

🏭

AIM's corporate and media interface. Organises guest lectures, workshops, panel discussions, and manages press releases and institutional branding.

✉ crc@aim-bhopal.org

Entrepreneurship Development Cell (E-Cell)

💡

Promotes entrepreneurial thinking through workshops, mentoring, consulting projects, and networking. Nurtures innovation and socially responsible entrepreneurship.

✉ edpcell@aim-bhopal.org

Students Academic Committee (SAC)

📚

The academic backbone of AIM. Coordinates lectures, assignments, academic schedules, and represents students in discussions with faculty and management.

✉ sac@aim-bhopal.org

Media Committee

📺

Manages communication between the institute and media outlets, ensuring consistent public visibility and effective internal communication across all channels.

✉ media@aim-bhopal.org
