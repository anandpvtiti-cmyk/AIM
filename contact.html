Getting Here

How to Reach Us

AIM is located at Barkheda Nathu, Neelbad, Bhopal and is well-connected to major transport hubs in the city.

✈️

By Air

Raja Bhoj Airport, Bhopal — approximately 25 km from AIM. Well-connected to Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, and Hyderabad via regular domestic flights.

From the airport, hire a taxi or use app-based cab services such as Ola or Uber to reach the institute conveniently.

🚆

By Rail

Bhopal Junction (Main Station) — approximately 15 km from AIM. A major stop on the Delhi–Mumbai and Delhi–Chennai railway routes.

Habibganj Railway Station — approximately 18 km from AIM. Serves express and superfast trains.

From both stations, hire auto-rickshaws, taxis, or use city bus services to reach AIM.

🚌

By Road

NH-46 connects Bhopal to Indore, Gwalior, and Nagpur. AIM is accessible via well-maintained state highways.

City Buses operate from ISBT Habibganj and Nadra Bus Stand, connected to the Neelbad area.

Private taxis and auto-rickshaws are readily available within the city.

For navigation, search "Anand Institute of Management, Bhopal" on Google Maps.

The City

About Bhopal

Bhopal, the capital of Madhya Pradesh, is a picturesque city that beautifully blends natural beauty, historical legacy, and modern development. Popularly known as the City of Lakes, it is famed for its stunning natural and man-made lakes.

Originally called Bhojpal after Raja Bhoj and later shaped by Dost Mohammed Khan in the 17th century, Bhopal today stands as a vibrant city celebrated for its rich heritage, architectural landmarks, scenic charm, and growing urban infrastructure.

AIM's location in Neelbad on the outskirts of Bhopal offers students a peaceful academic environment with easy access to the city's cultural, commercial, and recreational offerings.
