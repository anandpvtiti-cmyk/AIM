How to Apply

Admission Procedure

Selection is based on the CMET entrance examination conducted by AICTE, followed by DTE counselling. A merit-based, transparent process.

01

Appear in CMET Entrance Examination

The selection of students is based on the entrance examination held by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi — i.e., CMET (Common Management Entrance Test). Candidates are shortlisted on the basis of their quantitative abilities, logical reasoning skills, and verbal power.

02

DTE Merit List & Counselling

Final shortlisted candidates are called for counselling by the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Govt. of Madhya Pradesh, based on their merit. Candidates choose from institutes available in their merit category during DTE counselling sessions.

03

Seat Allotment & Reporting

After seat allotment through DTE counselling, candidates must report to AIM with all required documents. The institute verifies academic records, domicile certificates, category certificates (if applicable), and other documents as specified by DTE/University.

04

Fee Payment & Enrollment

Pay the semester fee (₹21,000 for Semester I including caution money) and complete the enrollment formalities. Students receive their admission confirmation, ID card, and student kit including uniform as part of the onboarding process.

05

Begin Your MBA Journey at AIM

Attend the induction programme (Parigraha) designed to introduce new students to AIM's academic culture, faculty, facilities, student clubs, and campus life. Your transformation into a business leader begins here.

Choose Your Path

MBA Specialisations

Six industry-aligned specialisations offered in Semester III. Choose the path that matches your career ambitions.

📈 Financial Management

Corporate finance, investment analysis, banking operations and financial decision-making for the modern business environment.

👓 Marketing Management

Consumer behaviour, brand management, digital marketing, sales strategy and market research methodologies.

👥 Human Resource Management

Talent acquisition, organisational behaviour, industrial relations, training & development and HR analytics.

🏭 Banking & Financial Service Management

Banking operations, credit management, financial institutions, insurance, and financial services industry overview.

🛍 Retail Management

Retail operations, merchandising, supply chain, visual merchandising, and managing retail personnel.

Tourism planning, hospitality management, destination marketing, and event & travel operations management.

In Semester IV, students choose electives from: International Business, Entrepreneurship Management, Rural & Urban Management, Event Management, Retail Management, and IT Management. This ensures a well-rounded business education tailored to individual career goals.
