Who We Are

A Dynamic B-School of Madhya Pradesh

Anand Institute of Management (AIM) has emerged as one of the fastest transforming and most dynamic B-Schools of Madhya Pradesh. Founded in 2015 under the Public Charitable Society — CHIEF, AIM is a benchmark of academic excellence and professional integrity.

The institute is guided and managed by former ministers, senior officers, and experienced academicians with exposure to premier institutions including IIMs, ensuring strong leadership and visionary governance.

AIM offers pioneering, industry-focused MBA programs designed to bridge the gap between academia and corporate expectations. Our corporate interface ensures practical exposure alongside rigorous academic learning.

  • Approved by AICTE, New Delhi
  • Recognised by DTE, Govt. of Madhya Pradesh
  • Affiliated to Barkatullah University, Bhopal
  • Annual intake of 180 students

The Organisation

Center of Human Initiative & Educational Furtherance

CHIEF is a distinguished voluntary, nonprofit social welfare and educational organisation, incorporated under the Madhya Pradesh Society Act.

Mission of CHIEF

CHIEF is dedicated to establishing and operating Technical Education Colleges, Business Management Institutes, Medical Education Colleges, and Research Centers. The society firmly believes that education is the most powerful instrument for empowering individuals and transforming society.

Through its institutions, CHIEF strives to deliver quality education and professional training that prepares students to meet the challenges of a dynamic and globally competitive environment.

Vision of CHIEF

To emerge as a pioneer in higher education and research by fostering academic excellence, innovative teaching methodologies, and a strong sense of social responsibility. We aspire to build institutions that nurture intellectual growth, professional competence, skill development, and strong ethical values among students.

Governance & Registration

The affairs of CHIEF are managed by an Executive Council comprising eminent individuals who bring vast experience across various fields. The organisation follows a democratic and transparent governance structure.

Our Obligations

Social Responsibility

At AIM, we believe education extends beyond the classroom. Our initiatives nurture a spirit of service, responsibility, and sustainable development among our students.

💻

Bridging the Digital Divide

AIM actively promotes computer literacy among those unfamiliar with digital tools, helping individuals explore new opportunities and build a better future.

🎉

Vocational Training

Every summer, AIM conducts free vocational training programs for economically disadvantaged children, equipping them with practical skills for sustainable livelihoods.

🌿

Environmental Sustainability

AIM organizes an annual Tree Plantation Drive on its Annual Day, promoting ecological awareness and contributing to building a greener planet.

💬

Spoken English & Personality

Regular Spoken English and Personality Development programs to enhance communication skills, confidence, and leadership abilities for global readiness.

🧠

Spiritual Enrichment

AIM invites spiritual leaders and motivational speakers to guide students and staff, fostering positivity, mindfulness, and strong ethical values.

❤

Prayas — Blood Donation

Every year on November 14th (Pt. Nehru's birthday), AIM organizes a Blood Donation Camp — 'Prayas' — reinforcing humanitarian service and social welfare.
