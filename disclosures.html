Student Safety

Anti-Ragging Policy

AIM maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against ragging in all its forms. Any incident of ragging is dealt with swiftly and decisively.

What Constitutes Ragging

›Any act causing physical or psychological harm to students
›Forcing students to do any act against their will
›Verbal abuse, teasing, or making derogatory remarks
›Any form of bullying, harassment, or victimisation
›Sexual harassment or gender-based discrimination

Anti-Ragging Helpline

UGC Helpline: 1800-180-5522

AIM Anti-Ragging Cell: +91-9111888339

Consequences & Action

›Immediate suspension from the institution
›Expulsion and cancellation of admission
›Withholding of scholarships and benefits
›FIR lodged with police as appropriate
›Debarment from examinations

Student Grievances

Grievance Redressal

AIM has a structured Grievance Redressal Mechanism to ensure that student concerns are addressed fairly, promptly, and transparently.

How to Lodge a Grievance

1

Submit Written Complaint

Write your grievance clearly and submit it to the Grievance Redressal Cell or the Director's office.

2

Acknowledgement

You will receive an acknowledgement within 3 working days of submission of your complaint.

3

Investigation & Resolution

The committee investigates the grievance and provides a resolution within 30 days.

4

Appeal

If unsatisfied, students can appeal to higher authorities including AICTE's online grievance portal.

Statutory Committees

Institutional Committees

AIM has constituted all mandatory statutory committees as per AICTE, UGC, and Government of India norms.

🚽 Anti-Ragging Committee

Constituted under UGC Regulations 2009 to prevent and address incidents of ragging. The committee conducts awareness programmes and handles complaints confidentially.

✉ antirag@aim-bhopal.org

👥 Student Grievance Redressal Committee

Addresses academic and administrative grievances of students. Ensures fair resolution of complaints within defined timelines as per AICTE norms.

✉ grievance@aim-bhopal.org

👩 Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)

Established under the POSH Act 2013. Handles complaints of sexual harassment at workplace and ensures a safe, respectful environment for all.

✉ icc@aim-bhopal.org

🌿 Prevention of Unfair Means Committee

Ensures academic integrity and ethical conduct in examinations, research, and all academic activities. Handles cases of malpractice and plagiarism.

✉ aim@aim-bhopal.org

🎓 Academic Council

The principal academic decision-making body responsible for curriculum, research policy, academic calendar, and quality assurance. Meets at least once per year.

✉ sac@aim-bhopal.org

📚 Board of Studies

Reviews and revises syllabi, recommends teaching methodologies, and ensures curriculum remains industry-relevant. Tenure of 4 years per constitution.

✉ aim@aim-bhopal.org
