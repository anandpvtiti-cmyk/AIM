The MBA (Full Time) 2-Year Programme prepares candidates for management careers in industry and services. Spread over four semesters, the programme offers core courses in the first year and specialisation options in the third semester.

The programme emphasises practical learning through case studies, field projects, business simulations, presentations, and interactive group activities to develop managerial and problem-solving skills.

How We Teach

Teaching Methodology

A student-centred, discovery-based approach that replaces conventional teacher-led instruction with experiential, peer, and industry-connected learning.

📚

Case Study Method

Real-world business cases analysed in syndicates. Students develop analytical ability, strategic thinking and decision-making through structured case discussions every semester.

🏆

Live Projects

Students collect, analyse and present industry data through live projects across various courses, bridging the gap between theory and corporate practice.

🏭

Industry Visits

Structured visits to IT, manufacturing, finance, services and marketing industries. Students submit project reports to faculty mentors post-visit for evaluation.

👥

Group Activities & Simulations

Syndicate discussions, management games, business simulations and role plays that develop teamwork, leadership and negotiation skills throughout the programme.

🎤

Guest Lectures & Seminars

Regular sessions by corporate leaders, banking professionals, NGO representatives, and public sector specialists, bringing real-world perspectives into the classroom.

🔮

Summer Internship

10-week mandatory training in reputed organisations between Semester I and III. Students work on defined projects under senior managers and submit detailed reports.

📋

Dissertation

Final semester dissertation under faculty supervision involving primary data collection, analytical research, and presentation before a panel of academic and industry experts.

💬

Personality Development

Extempore sessions, group discussions, mock interviews, aptitude tests, and workshops on communication, business ethics, and critical thinking every semester.

💻

Technology Integration

Add-on courses in Information Technology, Digital Marketing, and Business Analytics. Tools like SPSS, Python, Tableau, and Power BI integrated into coursework.

Continuous Evaluation Components

Class Participation

Evaluates commitment, research orientation, logical presentation, and communication skills through active discussion.

Projects & Industry

At least two applied learning activities per semester — GDs, seminars, quizzes, and industry-oriented projects.

Executive Education

MDPs & FDPs

Short-term executive programmes for working professionals, entrepreneurs, and faculty members. Expert-led, industry-relevant, and outcome-focused.

Management Development Programmes (MDPs)

Designed to enhance managerial competencies and leadership capabilities among professionals and executives.

  • Strategic thinking and decision-making
  • Expert-led sessions by domain specialists
